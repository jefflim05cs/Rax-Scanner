# Rax Scanner

Turn your iPhone into a pocket document scanner. Take photos of receipts, notes, forms or any paper document, and Rax Scanner turns them into clean, straight scans you can save to your Photos library.

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Author:** Jeff Lim

## What you can do

- **Scan documents with your camera.** Point your phone at a document and tap the shutter.
- **Get straight scans from any angle.** Rax Scanner finds the edges of the document for you. Drag any of the 4 corners to fine-tune them; a magnifier appears so you can place each corner exactly.
- **Check before you keep.** See the straightened scan, rotate it if needed, or retake the photo.
- **Scan in bulk.** Save as many scans as you like during a session without leaving the camera.
- **Review your scans.** Browse everything you've scanned, view each scan full-screen, rotate it, or delete the ones you don't want.
- **Save everything to Photos at once.** Export all your scans together as JPG (smaller files) or PNG (full quality).

## Getting started

1. Open Rax Scanner in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. When asked, allow access to your camera.

No app to install and no account needed.

## How to scan

1. **Take a photo.** Place the document on a flat surface, fit it inside the frame and tap the shutter button.
2. **Adjust the corners.** Check that the 4 corners sit on the corners of the document. Drag any that don't. Tap **Reset** to go back to the suggested corners. Tap **Next** when you're happy.
3. **Check the scan.** Tap **Rotate** to turn it, **Retake** to start over, or **Corners** to adjust the corners again. Tap **Save** to keep it.
4. **Repeat** for as many pages as you need. The button next to the shutter shows how many scans you've saved.

## Saving your scans to Photos

1. Tap the button next to the shutter to open your scans.
2. Tap **Export**.
3. Choose **JPG** or **PNG**, then tap **Export as JPG/PNG**.
4. When your scans are ready, tap **Save to Photos**.
5. In the share menu, choose **Save Images**.

## Good to know

- **Your scans are only kept while the page is open.** Refreshing or closing the page clears them, so export your scans before you leave.
- Your photos and scans stay on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The first time you scan, it may take a few seconds to find the document's edges. It's faster after that.
- For the best results, scan in good light, with the document on a surface that contrasts with the paper.
- If the corners aren't found automatically, you can always drag them into place yourself.

### Camera not working?

- Make sure camera access is allowed: tap **aA** in Safari's address bar › **Website Settings** › **Camera** › **Allow**, or go to **Settings › Safari › Camera**.
- Close any other app that might be using the camera, then tap **Try again**.
